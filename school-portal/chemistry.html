Curriculum Updates

Programme Structure & ITF

To comply with the NUC BMAS and accommodate the mandatory Industrial Training Fund (ITF) requirements, the curriculum is structured to optimize student success:

  • 100 Level: Students take designated PBB courses.
  • 300 Level (First Semester): Students register for a maximum of 26 credits. A minimum of 12 credits must be earned to progress to 400L (or 6 credits to remain on probation).
  • 300 Level (Second Semester): Dedicated entirely to the 6-month Industrial Training. No exams or carry-over courses are taken during this period.
  • Remaining 6 credits from 300L are pushed to 400L.
Admissions

4-Year Full-Time Programme

Candidates seeking admission must possess:

  • At least five ordinary credit passes in WASC, WAEC SSCE/GCE, NECO SSCE, or merit passes in TC II (max 2 sittings).
  • Required subjects include English Language, Mathematics, Chemistry, Physics, and Biology/Agricultural Science.
UME Subjects Use of English, Chemistry, Physics, Biology or Mathematics.
Direct Entry

3-Year Full-Time Programme

In addition to the O'Level requirements, Direct Entry candidates may be admitted with:

  • At least two A-level passes (GCE/HSC) in Chemistry and either Physics or Mathematics.
  • A credit pass in UNIBEN Diploma (DAIC, DSLT, DIG) or Upper Credit from recognized University Diplomas.
  • OND (Upper Credit) or HND (Lower Credit) in Science Laboratory Technology (SLT) or Polymer Chemistry.
  • NCE with credit level in Chemistry and Physics/Mathematics, plus an overall merit pass.
