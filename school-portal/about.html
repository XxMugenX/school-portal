About the Faculty

Faculty of Physical Sciences, University of Benin

You are welcome to the Faculty of Physical Sciences. The Faculty is as old as well as a new Faculty. An old Faculty because it was an integral part of the former Faculty of Science, which was the first Faculty in the University of Benin having started with the University in 1970. It is new in the sense that it is one of the two Faculties created out of the Faculty of Science in 2005.

Faculty identity

What the faculty stands for.

The Faculty of Physical Sciences is focused on teaching, research, and practical training across the disciplines that help students understand the natural world and apply science to real problems.

Mission To provide quality education, practical training, and research that address scientific and societal needs.
Vision To be a leading centre for physical sciences education and innovation in Nigeria.
Values Excellence, integrity, curiosity, discipline, and service.

Academic life

Teaching that connects theory to practice.

Students are guided through lectures, laboratory work, field studies, and project supervision. The goal is to graduate people who can think clearly, communicate well, and contribute confidently in industry, education, healthcare, and research.

Modern labs Support practical training and experimentation.
Research culture Encourages supervision, collaboration, and inquiry.
Career outcomes Prepares graduates for postgraduate and professional roles.
